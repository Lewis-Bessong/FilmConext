Gurren Lagann

Author: Hiroyuki Imaishi

Main Cast

Simon

The main protagonist , an orphan from Jeeha Village
who discovers the small mecha lagann. He grows from
a timid digger into the leader of Team Dai-Gurren with
alot of spiral energy.

Kamina

Simon's older brother figure , a charismatic and fearless
rebel who found Team Gurren and inspires humanity to break
through to the surface before falling in battle.
